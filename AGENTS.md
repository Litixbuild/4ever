## Project notes for AI assistants

4Everclips is a community site for World of Warcraft: Forever videos. Read README.md for the feature list and SETUP.md for how it is deployed.

- Next.js 16 App Router, **without** Cache Components (`cacheComponents` is off). Pages read cookies for the login, so they render per request. `params` and `searchParams` are Promises.
- Middleware is called **proxy** in this version: `src/proxy.ts`.
- Supabase: `src/lib/supabase/server.ts` acts as the visitor (security rules apply). `src/lib/supabase/admin.ts` uses the secret key and skips the rules; only use it on the server after checking the visitor with `getViewer()` / `requireCurator()`.
- Never let the browser decide a video's title, channel or creator. `lookupVideo()` reads them from YouTube or Twitch on the server.
- Title colors come from `src/lib/rarity.ts`. Never print tier names (Legendary, Epic, etc.) in the UI.
- Database changes go in a new file under `supabase/migrations/` with Row Level Security for any new table.
- The page backdrop (Alliance and Horde art) is `body::before` in `globals.css`, with images in `public/backdrop/`. The shade gradients there control how dim it is.
- Styling is plain CSS in `src/app/globals.css`. Reuse its classes (`btn`, `card`, `seg`, `chip`, `clip-grid`, ...) before adding new ones.
- The look borrows from the game's UI: `.wow-tip` is the item tooltip (title in a quality color, green "Use:" and "<Made by>" lines, gold flavor text), `.slot` is the empty equipment slot used for empty states, `CharacterTip` shows a player's main the way the unit tooltip does, and `.btn-wow` is the red dialog button.
- The logo is `Logo` in `src/components/brand.tsx`: the map-filled 4 (`public/brand/four-*.webp`, made from Cameron's artwork) plus EVER in Metamorphous, with EV glowing red and ER glowing blue (`.glow-*` in `globals.css`, animations off for reduced motion).
- Display text is Marcellus with Cinzel's digits swapped in (the `4Ever Figures` face in `globals.css`, file in `src/fonts/`), because Marcellus draws 0 and 1 like O and l. Keep `--font-display` pointing at that stack.
- Login buttons come from `enabledProviders()` in `src/lib/auth-providers.ts`, which reads which providers are switched on in Supabase. Don't hard-code the list of login options in pages.
- `/watch` is the full-screen swipe feed (`src/components/clip-feed.tsx`); on phones the Clips menu link opens it. Clips come in a fresh random order made in the browser (up to 300 clips; a `?start=` clip plays first). Clips already watched are remembered in localStorage (`4ever:seen-clips`, helpers in `src/lib/seen.ts`; cards and the video page mark clips too, and cards show a "Watched" chip via `SeenMark`) and left out; after the last one the feed says "You've seen every clip". Swipe up (or scroll the wheel down) for the next clip, down (wheel up) for the previous; a click or tap on the video pauses it; clips autoplay with sound (muted only when the browser requires it: a visitor who hasn't clicked anything yet, or an iPhone). On phones the clip fills the screen; a clear layer over it reads swipes and taps (tap to pause). Twitch only autoplays a player that is at least 400×300, fully visible and not moved by CSS transforms or animations, and never autoplays on phones (one tap needed). So Twitch clips skip the slide-in animation, are never cropped or covered, and on phones sit full width in the middle; desktop has up/down buttons (`.feed-arrows`) because a Twitch player keeps the mouse wheel. Front-page activity says "X shared" only when X really posted it.
- Contests are on hold: `/contests` is a coming-soon page and `/contests/[slug]` redirects there. The contest code (actions, widgets, admin) is kept for later.
- Profiles follow WoW: Forever: characters have a first and last name (saved together in `main_character`), the `realm` column holds the ruleset (PvP, PvE, RP-PvP, Hardcore) since there are no realms, and `main_spec` holds the spec. Level is no longer asked for.
- Clips store the channel's own picture (`channel_avatar_url`, read from YouTube or Twitch at submit time). Credit lines use the creator's 4Ever avatar first, then that picture. Older clips get it filled in the first time their page is opened.
- Run `npm test`, `npm run typecheck` and `npm run lint` before committing.
- Deleting a video happens on your own profile: under each card in the Made and Shared tabs (`ClipGrid deletable`). The server action `deleteClip` checks the viewer is the creator or the person who shared it.
- First login shows "Choose your class" (`src/components/class-picker.tsx`, mounted for logged-in visitors by `RollPrompt` in the root layout): class, then spec, saved by `saveRoll` in `src/app/actions/roll.ts`. It opens by itself only when `main_class` and `class_prompted_at` are both empty; "Not now" sets `class_prompted_at`. Your own profile has a button that reopens it. Class emblems are in `public/classes/` (supplied by Cameron), spec blurbs in `SPEC_INFO` in `src/lib/wow.ts`.
- /browse shows 9 a page as a 3×3 grid (`ClipGrid threeUp`) with just Previous / Next page (`?page=`): it never shows a total count or page numbers, so the site never looks small.
- Site shares: a curator posting someone else's clip can tick "Post as 4Ever" (on by default); `clips.shared_as_site` hides the curator's name so the clip shows "Shared by 4Ever", and it stays off the curator's public profile (their own profile still lists it so they can delete it).
- Titles: curators get "Edit title" on a clip page (`renameClip`); it sets `title_locked` so the daily refresh never overwrites the fix. New titles go through `titleNeedsReview` (src/lib/moderation.ts); a flagged title is held for review even when a curator submits it.
- Desktop top nav is just Movies and Clips (no Contests button until contests return; announce them with a banner). On phones (≤760px) the same nav becomes two tabs across under the logo, Clips first (`.nav-first`, links to /watch) with icons (`.nav-icon`), and the header Submit is hidden. `MobileTabBar` (src/components/mobile-tab-bar.tsx, `.dock`) pins the experience bar to the bottom with a small gold rounded-square Submit button floating above it on the right. The swipe feed covers it. The Creators and Hall of Fame pages still exist but aren't linked from the nav or footer.
- Suggestions: logged-in visitors send ideas, bugs or creator requests at /suggest (linked from the footer, the account menu, and the feed's end card). They live in `public.suggestions` (server-only, admin client). Curators answer them at /admin/suggestions (Open / Answered / Closed); the reply shows under the suggestion on /suggest. A red dot on the header avatar means an unread reply (or, for curators, open suggestions); opening /suggest marks replies read.
- Security: `src/lib/supabase/admin.ts` and `src/lib/suggestions-server.ts` import `server-only`, so the build fails if a client component pulls them in; keep browser-safe helpers in separate files. The login page only shows messages from `LOGIN_ERRORS` (`?error=<code>`), never text from the link. Signed-in users can insert only `clip_id, user_id` (+ `body` for comments) into votes/favorites/comments, so `created_at` can't be back-dated past the comment rate limit. Logged-out views count once per connection (`x-nf-client-connection-ip`) per day.
- Watch-time ranks: 1 XP per minute watched (`profiles.xp_minutes`), ranks and curve in `src/lib/ranks.ts` (the 14 original PvP ranks, Horde or Alliance names by `profiles.faction`; "Rank N" without a faction; name colors climb like item quality). The browser counts seconds only while a video plays and the tab is visible (`src/lib/watch-xp.ts`: the feed, `VideoPlayer` and `InlinePlayer` call `setWatching`, each video earning for about its own length) and posts each full minute to `POST /api/xp`, which calls the server-only `award_watch_minute()` (one per 50 s, `DAILY_XP_CAP` a day). `XpBar` shows it under the phone tab bar, under the desktop header and as a thin line in the feed; `RankToast` announces rank-ups; profiles show a Rank section (`RankList`).
- `/help` ("How 4Ever works", linked from the account menu, the footer, the submit page and a strip at the bottom of the front page) explains credit, sharing, claiming, watching, ranks (table built from `src/lib/ranks.ts`), title colors (from `RARITY_TIERS`), community features, accounts and removal. Keep it in step when those features change. The front page no longer has the Top creators card or the credit band; that walkthrough lives on /help.
- Logins only work on https://4ever.watch (each address keeps its own login cookies). `redirects()` in next.config.ts sends every other address there: wc4ever.netlify.app, any `*--wc4ever.netlify.app` deploy or branch address, and www. The proxy forwards a stray `/?code=` (Supabase's Site URL fallback) to /auth/callback, and the callback carries on if the browser is already logged in.
- Faster video starts: `src/lib/warm.ts` preconnects to YouTube or Twitch player servers when someone points at or taps a video, opens a video page, or a clip is next in the feed. Preconnect only (no prefetch), because the privacy page promises players load only after play is pressed.
- Player-made: `clips.player_made` puts a gold "Player-made" mark (`PlayerMadeMark`, src/components/player-made.tsx) on cards, clip pages and the feed. It's set automatically for movies submitted with "I made this"; curators toggle it on any clip page (`setPlayerMade`). The front page: launch bar, then `HomeStage` (src/components/home-stage.tsx). Its queue starts with the three most watched videos today (`getMostWatchedToday` → `most_watched` RPC over `clip_views`; the day resets at midnight Eastern, and views are recorded under the Eastern date; gaps early in the day are filled by the admin's feature, the top movie and the newest). #1 is the Featured Video (gold "Featured Video" heading with embers, gold title), #2 has a purple title, #3 blue (`rank-gold/purple/blue`); no "2nd/3rd" wording. The Featured Video is only in the big player; the rail under it starts with #2, then #3, then the newest (a "Back to the Featured Video" button shows while another plays). The rail is swipeable (`.stage-rail`, arrows on desktop) so people can watch in any order. Pressing play on the big poster or any rail card opens the full-screen player (`.theater`: fixed over the page, asks the browser for fullscreen too, starts by itself, X or Escape closes it, "Play next video" under the player). Nothing may cover the player or move it with a transform (Twitch). YouTube moves on by itself when a video ends, Twitch waits for a click. Below (src/components/home-boards.tsx): the Faction war bar (`faction_war` RPC: minutes watched this week per faction from `faction_minutes`, which `award_watch_minute` fills; red/blue sparks and a pulsing gold seam at the front line), category tiles (World PvP, Battlegrounds, Raids & Dungeons, Funny; art = that category's top thumbnail), the Made by players shelf (real `player_made` videos, gold + button to /submit). Nothing after that: no video grid or Latest activity on the front page; full lists are under the Movies and Clips tabs. The info card beside the big player must not overlap it (Twitch).
- Upload dates: `clips.uploaded_at` is when the video went up on YouTube or Twitch (`uploadedAt` from the lookups, set on submit; the daily refresh fills it in for older videos, picking up any with it missing). Cards, clip pages, the big player and the feed show it as "3 days ago" (`uploadedAgo`). "Newest" everywhere sorts by `uploaded_at` (then `published_at`); the clips feed plays newest first (no shuffle), leaving out watched clips.
